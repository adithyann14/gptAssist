claudeAssist is a simple WebView wrapper for Claude (claude.ai).

- Multiple accounts, each with its own separate cookies and site data
- Unrestricted by default; each account can be switched to restricted mode, which blocks all URLs not essential for functioning

Tap the hand icon at the top right to toggle restricted / unrestricted.
Long press the hand icon to switch, add, rename or delete accounts.
Swipe up a bit to hide the button.
